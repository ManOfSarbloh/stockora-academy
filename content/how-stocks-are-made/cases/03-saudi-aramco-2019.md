On **5 December 2019**, Saudi Aramco announced the end of its bookbuilding and a final offer price of **SAR 32** per share, the top of its range. The facts from the company's announcement:

| | |
|---|---|
| Shares offered (before over-allotment) | **3,000,000,000**, or **1.5%** of the share capital |
| Offer amount | **SAR 96.0 billion / USD 25.6 billion** |
| Total demand | **SAR 446 billion / USD 119 billion**, or **465%** of the shares offered |
| Split | **33.3%** of the offer shares to individual investors, **66.7%** to institutions |
| Over-allotment (greenshoe) option | up to **450,000,000** extra shares (**15%**), granted by the selling shareholder |
| If the option is used in full | **3,450,000,000** shares, **SAR 110.4 billion / USD 29.4 billion** |

The shares started trading on the Saudi Exchange (Tadawul) on **11 December 2019**.

**What it shows:** "size" depends on what you measure. The offer raised a very large amount of money, yet the public bought only a small slice of the company. Demand of more than four times the shares offered let the price be set at the top of the range, and the greenshoe was 15% of the offer, the same limit EU rules set (step 5).
