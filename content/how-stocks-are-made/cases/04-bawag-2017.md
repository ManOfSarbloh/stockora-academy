The Austrian bank BAWAG Group AG went public on the **prime market** of the Vienna Stock Exchange on **25 October 2017**. The Vienna Stock Exchange called it the largest IPO in its history, ahead of Strabag (2007, €1.3 billion), Raiffeisen International (2005, €1.1 billion) and Telekom Austria (2000, €1 billion).

| | |
|---|---|
| Price range | **€47 to €52** |
| Offer price | **€48** |
| Shares placed | **40,250,000 existing shares**: 35,000,000 plus **5,250,000** over-allotment shares (15%) |
| Proceeds (incl. greenshoe) | about **€1.93 billion**, for the **selling shareholders**; BAWAG itself received no proceeds |
| Total shares / free float | **100,000,000** shares, **39.9%** free float |
| First price on the exchange | **€47.40** |
| Market capitalisation at the start of trading | **€4.7 billion** |

Two days after its debut, on **27 October 2017**, BAWAG joined the **ATX** under the index's fast-entry rule.

**What it shows:**

- **Secondary shares only:** all the money went to the existing owners, none to the company. Always check the prospectus to see who receives the money.
- **No "pop":** the shares were priced near the bottom of the range and first traded slightly **below** the offer price (€47.40 vs €48). Not every IPO jumps on day one.
- **The greenshoe** was 15% of the base offer, the maximum allowed under EU rules.
