This policy describes how the site is built. The site owner must review it (ideally with legal advice) before the site goes public, especially the hosting section.

## What we do not do

- No analytics or statistics tools.
- No cookies.
- No advertising, no affiliate links, no social media buttons or tracking pixels.
- No accounts or sign-ups. We do not ask for your name or email address.
- Fonts are served from this website itself, not from a third-party font service.

## What is stored in your own browser

To remember your settings and learning progress, the site uses your browser's *local storage*. This data stays on your device; it is never sent to us or anyone else. All keys start with `stockora-`. Currently stored:

- `stockora-theme`: whether you chose light or dark mode.
- `stockora-progress`: which lessons you marked as done, and when.
- `stockora-bookmarks`: lessons you bookmarked.
- `stockora-quiz-scores`: your best and latest score for each quiz.
- `stockora-quiz-stats`: for each quiz question you answered, how often you got it right and when it is due again (used by the review and weak-spot modes).
- `stockora-quiz-daily`: your daily quiz streak.
- `stockora-quiz-exam`: your Final Exam attempts, best score and pass date.
- `stockora-quiz-name`: the name you typed for your certificate, if any.
- `stockora-founder-game`: whether you finished the founder game on the "How Stocks Are Made" page, and when.
- `stockora-times-zone`: the time zone you chose on the Trading Times page.

This list will be kept up to date. You can delete all of it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

## Videos (YouTube)

Lesson and topic pages include educational YouTube videos. A video does **not** load until you click on it, and before that no data (not even a preview image) is requested from YouTube. Only then does your browser connect to YouTube (using the privacy-enhanced `youtube-nocookie.com` domain), and YouTube's own privacy policy applies. Every video also has a plain "Watch on YouTube" link instead.

## Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web server, the hosting provider receives technical data needed to deliver pages (for example your IP address and browser information) and may log it, for example for security purposes. We do not have access to visitor logs.

The site owner must check GitHub's current terms and privacy statement for GitHub Pages, and add the required details here (provider, purpose, legal basis, data transfers).
