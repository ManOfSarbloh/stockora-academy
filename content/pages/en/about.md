## What Stockora is

Stockora Academy explains how the stock market works, from what a single share is, to how companies go public, to how markets in different countries differ. It is written in plain English for complete beginners (16+) and goes all the way to advanced topics. Every lesson aims to define its terms, show a worked example, and point to official sources.

The lessons, quiz questions and country pages are in English. The interface, tools, glossary, Scam Shield and these information pages are also available in German, Spanish, French and Italian (use the language menu in the header).

Austria is our "home" example, so you will find Austrian notes throughout, next to examples from other countries.

## What "Academy" means here

**"Academy" simply means "a place to learn".** Stockora Academy is **not** an accredited school, university or training institution, and it does **not** award recognised qualifications. It is also **not** a bank, broker, investment adviser or any other regulated financial service.

## Our rules

- **Education only.** No investment, tax or legal advice. No buy or sell recommendations.
- **No products pushed.** No recommended stocks, funds, brokers, apps or crypto. No affiliate links. No ads.
- **No tracking.** No analytics, no cookies, no third-party trackers. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).
- **Sources and dates.** Factual pages list their sources and when they were last reviewed. See [Sources & Methodology](/sources/).
- **Honest about risk.** Investing can lose money. We say so clearly and often.
