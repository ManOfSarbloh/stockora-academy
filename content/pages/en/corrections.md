## How to report a mistake

Every quiz question and lesson will have a "Report a mistake" link. When you report something, please include:

- the page address (URL),
- what is wrong, and
- if possible, an official source that shows the correct information.

The reporting channel (a GitHub issue form or an email address) will be added when the site is published.
