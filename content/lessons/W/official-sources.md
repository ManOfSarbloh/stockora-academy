Most of what you read about a company or the economy is second-hand. A journalist, an app or a forum post summarises something that was first published somewhere official. Going back to that original is the best protection against errors, exaggeration and manipulation.

## Company information

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| **Annual and half-year reports** | The company's investor relations (IR) website (Category I) |
| **Ad hoc announcements** (inside information) | Published by the company; listed issuers must publish inside information without delay (FMA; Category R) |
| **Regulated information of Austrian listed issuers** | OeKB, the **Officially Appointed Mechanism (OAM)** for Austria, receives the regulated information of listed issuers |
| **US companies' filings** | The SEC's **EDGAR** database, searchable for free (annual reports on Form 10-K and more) |
| **General meeting documents** | The company's website, published with the notice (Category V) |

> [!TIP] Read the announcement, not just the headline
> When news says "Company X raises outlook", open the company's own announcement. The exact wording, numbers and conditions are there, and often more cautious than the headline.

## Checking a provider

- The **FMA company database** shows which companies hold an Austrian licence and what they may do.
- The **FMA warning list** names providers that are not authorised (Category T).

Use both before sending money to any firm you don't know.

## Economic data

Official statistics come from statistical offices and central banks:

- **Statistik Austria** for Austrian inflation, growth and employment (Category L);
- **Eurostat** for euro area data, with a public **release calendar** showing when each figure comes out;
- the **ECB** for interest rate decisions and exchange rates (Category L).

## Laws and rules

- **RIS** (*Rechtsinformationssystem des Bundes*) publishes Austrian federal law in its current version, free of charge. Many lessons on this site cite it directly.
- **EUR-Lex** publishes EU law, such as the Market Abuse Regulation (Category R).

## Key takeaways

> [!TAKEAWAYS]
> - Most financial news is second-hand; the original is usually published officially.
> - Company reports and announcements are on IR websites; OeKB is Austria's OAM for regulated information.
> - EDGAR gives free access to US company filings.
> - The FMA company database and warning list help you check providers.
> - Official statistics come from Statistik Austria, Eurostat and the ECB; laws from RIS and EUR-Lex.
