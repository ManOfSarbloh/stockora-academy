## Four kinds of content

| Type | Purpose | Typical signs |
|---|---|---|
| **News report** | Report what happened | Facts, sources, quotes from several sides |
| **Analysis or commentary** | Explain or argue a view | Author's opinion, "we think", forecasts |
| **Paid content** | Promote a product for money | "Anzeige", "Werbung", "sponsored", "in cooperation with" |
| **Marketing communication** | A firm promoting its own services | Comes from the provider; aims to get you to act |

All four can be useful, but you should know which one you're reading.

## What Austrian law requires

> [!AUSTRIA] Labelling paid content
> - **Periodical media:** announcements, recommendations and other contributions for whose publication **payment** is made must be labelled **"Anzeige"**, **"entgeltliche Einschaltung"** or **"Werbung"**, unless the design or placement rules out any doubt that they are paid (§ 26 Mediengesetz).
> - **Investment firms:** all information to clients, including marketing communications, must be **fair, clear and not misleading**, and **marketing communications must be clearly identifiable as such** (§ 49 WAG 2018).

These rules help, but they don't cover everything: a post by an anonymous account on social media may be paid without anyone knowing. The FMA advises asking who is behind a finfluencer's recommendation and whether they have a financial interest, for example through affiliate marketing (Category O).

## Questions to ask

1. **Who wrote it, and who pays them?** A journalist, an analyst at a bank that sells the product, a company press office, an influencer with affiliate links?
2. **Is it labelled?** Look for "Anzeige", "Werbung", "sponsored", "advertorial", "Marketingmitteilung".
3. **Is it reporting or predicting?** Facts can be checked; forecasts are opinions.
4. **Are there sources?** Can you find the original announcement or data (lesson 2)?
5. **Is there a call to action?** "Buy now", "limited places", "only today" point to marketing, or worse (Category T).

> [!EXAMPLE] Three texts about the same share (fictional)
> - **News:** "Alpenrad AG reported a 4% rise in sales for the first half, according to its half-year report published on Tuesday."
> - **Commentary:** "In our view, Alpenrad is undervalued and could rise 30%."
> - **Paid content (labelled "Werbung"):** "Discover Alpenrad's growth story - open a trading account now and get a bonus!"

## Key takeaways

> [!TAKEAWAYS]
> - News, commentary, paid content and marketing can look alike; identify which you're reading.
> - In Austria, paid content in periodical media must be labelled "Anzeige", "entgeltliche Einschaltung" or "Werbung".
> - Investment firms' marketing communications must be clearly identifiable and not misleading.
> - Social media posts may be paid without a label; ask who benefits.
> - Prefer reports with sources you can check; treat forecasts as opinions.
