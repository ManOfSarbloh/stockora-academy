Stock exchanges are not open around the clock. Each has fixed **trading hours** in its own local time, from Monday to Friday, except on holidays. Outside these hours, you can usually still enter orders with your broker, but they wait until the market opens.

## A trading day in Vienna

> [!AUSTRIA]
> For ATX shares on the Vienna Stock Exchange, a normal trading day looks like this (Vienna time):
>
> | Phase | Time |
> |---|---|
> | Pre-trading (orders can be entered, nothing trades) | 08:00 – 08:55 |
> | Opening auction | 08:55 – 09:00 |
> | Continuous trading | 09:00 – 12:00 |
> | Intraday auction | 12:00 – 12:03 |
> | Continuous trading | 12:03 – 17:30 |
> | Closing auction | 17:30 – 17:35 |
> | Trade at close (at the closing price) | 17:35 – 17:45 |
> | Post-trading | until 17:50 |
>
> Other segments have slightly different times, and less liquid shares may only trade in auctions.

## A trading day in New York

At the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE), the **core trading session** runs from **9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Eastern Time**, starting with an opening auction and ending with a closing auction at 4:00 p.m. Before and after that, there are extended sessions (next lesson).

## Why trading hours matter

- **Execution:** an order you place at 22:00 Vienna time on a Vienna-listed share will not be executed until the next morning, possibly at a quite different price.
- **News:** company news often comes out before the open or after the close. The price then "gaps" at the next open (Category J explains gaps on charts).
- **Foreign shares:** a US share bought through a European broker may trade on a European exchange during European hours, but its main market is open only in the afternoon and evening, European time.

> [!EXAMPLE]
> You live in Vienna and want to buy shares of a company listed only in New York. The NYSE core session runs from 9:30 to 16:00 New York time, which is normally **15:30 to 22:00 in Vienna**. If you place your order at 10:00 Vienna time, it may wait more than five hours before the main market opens.

> [!VERIFY]
> Trading hours change from time to time. Always check the official page of the exchange; the Trading Times section of this site will show live open/closed status.

> [!MISTAKE] "My broker app is open, so the market is open"
> Many apps let you place orders 24 hours a day, but that does not mean the exchange is trading. Check whether your order goes to a market that is open, and at what price it might execute when the market opens.

## Key takeaways

> [!TAKEAWAYS]
> - Each exchange has fixed hours in local time, Monday to Friday.
> - Vienna: opening auction from 08:55, continuous trading 09:00–17:30 (with a short midday auction), closing auction 17:30–17:35.
> - NYSE core session: 9:30–16:00 New York time, normally 15:30–22:00 in Vienna.
> - Orders placed outside trading hours wait for the next session.
